Welcome to TGVIS

Where Learning Meets Inspiration

The Green Valley International School is a learning community in Bihta, Patna, where children are encouraged to ask questions, take part, and grow at their own pace.

From Nursery to Class 10, the school brings together classroom learning, co-curricular activities, sports, and values-led routines. A separate tuition programme also supports families looking for additional academic help.

Why Choose Us

The TGVIS Advantage

Discover what makes The Green Valley International School the preferred choice for parents in Bihta and beyond.

Academic Excellence

A structured school programme with clear learning goals, regular practice, and support for children across Nursery to Class 10.

Sports & Fitness

Regular movement, games, sports, and fitness activities help children build confidence, teamwork, and healthy routines.

Safe & Secure

A respectful school culture, clear routines, and an open line to the school office help children feel supported and known.

Expert Faculty

Teachers guide children with patience, explain concepts clearly, and encourage students to take ownership of their learning.

Holistic Development

Art, music, speaking, celebrations, and community activities give students room to express themselves beyond the classroom.

Integrated Tuition

A separate tuition programme gives families an additional route for revision, doubt-clearing, and focused academic practice.

Our Programs

Nurturing Growth at Every Stage

From the earliest years to board examinations, our tailored programs ensure age-appropriate learning and development.

Early Childhood Education

Our Nursery and Kindergarten programs create a magical, play-based learning environment where children aged 3-5 develop foundational skills through creative exploration, storytelling, sensory activities, and social interaction.

  • Play-based learning methodology
  • Montessori-inspired activities
  • Introduction to English, Hindi & Mathematics
  • Art, Music & Physical Development
  • Safe, colorful, child-friendly classrooms
  • Dedicated trained early-childhood educators
Learn More

Primary School (Class 1-5)

Building strong academic foundations with a structured yet creative approach. Students develop reading fluency, numerical skills, scientific curiosity, and social awareness through hands-on learning experiences.

  • Comprehensive school curriculum
  • English, Hindi, Mathematics, EVS, Computer Science
  • Smart classroom technology integration
  • Activity-based & experiential learning
  • Regular assessments with parent feedback
  • Library & reading programs
Learn More

Middle School (Class 6-8)

An exciting phase where students explore deeper concepts in science, mathematics, and humanities. Our middle school program encourages critical thinking, project-based learning, and discovery of personal interests.

  • Deeper concepts with lab work
  • Science, Mathematics, Social Studies, Languages
  • Fully equipped Science & Computer Labs
  • Project-based and collaborative learning
  • Olympiad preparation & competitive exam coaching
  • Personality development & public speaking
Learn More

Secondary School (Class 9-10)

Preparing students for their secondary examinations with focused academic coaching, integrated tuition support, and career guidance. The programme builds confidence and readiness for higher education.

  • Secondary examination preparation
  • Science, Commerce stream guidance
  • Integrated tuition & doubt-clearing sessions
  • Mock tests & exam strategy workshops
  • Career counselling & higher education guidance
  • Exam preparation, revision & career conversations
