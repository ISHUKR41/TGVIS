---
name: Verified contact flows
description: Durable rules for public contact, group links, and unverified school data.
---

Public school actions should use only contact details and links that the school has supplied or that have been verified. When a private class-group invite URL is unavailable, open a pre-filled WhatsApp request to the school office instead of inventing an invite link. Keep unverified email addresses, statistics, dates, fees, and authentication claims clearly qualified.

**Why:** A polished school website must not send families to fake groups or present placeholder institutional information as fact.

**How to apply:** Before adding a public CTA, check whether the destination was supplied or verified. Prefer the school office phone/WhatsApp handoff for missing operational details, and replace temporary wording only after confirmation.
