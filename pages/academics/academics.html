Our Approach

Academic Philosophy

At TGVIS, learning is presented as more than textbooks and exams. The school describes a programme that brings together classroom learning, activities, critical thinking, and values.

Optional academic support may be available within the school. Families should confirm the current curriculum, affiliation, timetable, and tuition arrangements with the office.

Our Programs

Academic Levels

Pre-Primary (Nursery - UKG)

Play-based learning with Montessori elements. Focus on motor skills, language, numeracy, creativity, and social interaction in a safe, colorful environment.

  • ✓ Activity-based curriculum
  • ✓ Rhymes, stories & art
  • ✓ Outdoor play & physical development

Primary (Class 1-5)

Foundation building through language, numeracy, environmental learning, creative work, and age-appropriate activities. Confirm the current subject list with the office.

  • ✓ Age-appropriate learning
  • ✓ Activity-based support
  • ✓ Regular parent-teacher meetings

Middle School (Class 6-8)

Deep exploration with lab work and project-based learning. Science, Mathematics, Social Studies, Languages, Computer Science with competitive exam preparation.

  • ✓ Science & Computer Labs
  • ✓ Olympiad preparation
  • ✓ Personality development programs

Secondary (Class 9-10)

Focused board-exam preparation for Classes 9–10 covering all core subjects with special coaching sessions, weekly tests, and individual attention to help students achieve their best results.

  • ✓ Board exam coaching
  • ✓ Weekly mock tests & revision
  • ✓ Career guidance sessions
Infrastructure

Learning Facilities

Smart Classrooms

Interactive digital boards, projectors, and multimedia learning tools in every classroom for engaging visual education.

Science Laboratory

Fully equipped Physics, Chemistry, and Biology labs for hands-on experiments and practical understanding of concepts.

Computer Lab

Modern computer lab with high-speed internet for digital literacy, coding, and ICT skills development.

Library

Well-stocked library with thousands of books, periodicals, and digital resources to foster a reading culture.

Dedicated space for art, music, dance, and other creative activities that nurture artistic talent.
