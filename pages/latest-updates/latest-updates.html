TGVIS Class 10 Students Score 100% in CBSE Board Exams

August 1, 2025 TGVIS Administration

We are incredibly proud to announce that every single student from TGVIS Class 10 has passed the CBSE Board Examinations for the 2024-25 session. Multiple students scored above 90%, with our topper achieving an outstanding aggregate. This result reflects the hard work of our students, the dedication of our teachers, and the support of every TGVIS parent. We congratulate the entire school community on this achievement.
