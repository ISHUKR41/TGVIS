A Day at TGVIS

From morning assembly to the final bell, every moment at TGVIS is designed to inspire, educate, and empower.

Morning Assembly

Every day begins with prayer, national anthem, thought of the day, and important announcements — setting a positive tone for the day.

Interactive Classrooms

Smart classrooms with digital boards, group discussions, hands-on activities, and personalised attention from dedicated teachers.

Lab & Practicals

Fully equipped science and computer labs where students experiment, code, and discover the joy of hands-on learning.

Sports & Play

Dedicated sports periods and activity options may include cricket, football, basketball, athletics, yoga, and indoor games. Confirm the current coaching and schedule with the school office.

Creative Arts

Art, music, dance, and drama sessions nurture creativity and self-expression. Regular performances showcase student talent.

Lunch & Recreation

A clean, supervised dining experience with nutritious food options. Recreational time for socializing and relaxation.

Our Campus

World-Class Facilities

Our modern campus is designed to provide a safe, stimulating, and comfortable learning environment for every student.

Classroom Learning

Ask the school office which classrooms currently use digital boards, projectors, or other audio-visual learning tools.

Safeguarding & Supervision

Families can ask the school office about current visitor procedures, supervision, safeguarding, emergency, and child-protection systems.

Transport Enquiry

Transport routes, vehicles, pick-up points, safety arrangements, and fees vary by session. Confirm current availability with the office.

Health & First Aid

Ask the school office about current first-aid, illness, emergency, health-check, and parent-contact arrangements.

Library & Reading Room

A library, reading resources, and a quiet study area can support learning beyond the classroom. Ask the office about the current collection and access.

Playground & Sports Ground

A spacious playground for cricket, football, basketball, and athletics. Separate areas for junior and senior students.

Exclusive Feature

Integrated Tuition Program

Extra Academic Support — Built Right In

Unlike other schools, TGVIS offers an integrated tuition facility within the school campus. Students who need extra help or want to practice more can attend supervised tuition sessions after regular school hours.

  • Doubt-clearing sessions for all subjects
  • Exam preparation and revision classes
  • Dedicated tutors for weak subject areas
  • Safe campus environment — no need to travel
  • Available for Class 1 to Class 10 students
