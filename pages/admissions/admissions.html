How to Apply

Simple Admission Process

Our admission process is transparent, simple, and designed to welcome every student with warmth.

01

Enquiry & Visit

Visit our campus or contact us to learn about our programs. Take a campus tour and meet our faculty. Walk-ins welcome during office hours.

02

Application Form

Collect the admission form from the school office or download it below. Fill in the required details and attach necessary documents.

03

Document Submission

Submit the completed form along with birth certificate, Aadhar card, previous school records, passport photos, and address proof.

04

Interaction & Assessment

A brief, age-appropriate interaction/assessment to understand the student's learning level. For Nursery, this is an informal interaction.

05

Confirmation & Enrollment

Upon selection, complete the fee payment to confirm admission. Collect the welcome kit with uniform details, timetable, and school handbook.

Documents Required

Academic Documents

  • Previous School Report Card
  • Transfer Certificate (TC)
  • Character Certificate
  • Migration Certificate (if applicable)

Other Documents

  • Address Proof (Electricity Bill / Ration Card)
  • Caste Certificate (if applicable)
  • Income Certificate (for scholarship)
  • Medical Fitness Certificate
