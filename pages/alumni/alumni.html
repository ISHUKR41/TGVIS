Ways to Stay Connected

Being an alumnus is more than a title — it's an active commitment to the school that helped shape who you are.

Register as Alumni

Join our official alumni database. Stay updated on reunions, events, and new opportunities at TGVIS. Your profile helps us connect you with former classmates.

Mentor Students

Share your career journey with current students through mentoring sessions, guest lectures, and career talks. Your experience is invaluable guidance for the next generation.

Annual Alumni Meet

Join our annual reunion on campus. Reconnect with teachers and batchmates, walk through the corridors you grew up in, and make new memories together.

Support Scholarships

Contribute to the TGVIS scholarship fund and help a deserving student get the same quality education that shaped your life. Every contribution matters.

Alumni Sports Tournament

Join our annual cricket and football alumni tournament. Relive those Sports Day memories while competing against current students and fellow alumni.

Alumni Newsletter

Subscribe to our alumni newsletter for school news, achievement spotlights, reunion announcements, and stories from fellow TGVIS graduates.
