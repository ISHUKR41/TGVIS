Value for Money

What Your Fee Covers

Your child's fee funds a complete learning experience — not just textbooks, but everything that makes TGVIS special.

Academic Programme

Full CBSE curriculum delivery, smart classroom access, laboratory sessions, library membership, and digital learning resources.

Integrated Tuition

In-school doubt-clearing sessions, revision classes, mock tests, and personalised coaching — no external tuition needed.

Sports & Activities

Cricket, football, basketball, athletics, yoga, and indoor games with professional coaching. All club activities and cultural programmes.

Infrastructure Access

Smart classrooms, computer lab, science lab, library, activity hall, and sports ground — all included in your fee.

Safety & Supervision

24/7 CCTV, trained security staff, daily supervision, medical room access, and regular health check-ups.

Parent Engagement

PTMs, progress reports, WhatsApp group updates, and school events — keeping you connected to your child's journey.

Pay Your Way

Payment Options

Cash

Pay at the school accounts office during working hours (Mon–Sat, 8 AM–3 PM).

Bank Transfer

Direct NEFT/RTGS to the school bank account. Contact the office for account details.

UPI / QR Code

Scan our UPI QR code at the office for instant digital payment via any UPI app.

Cheque / DD

Cheque or demand draft in favour of "The Green Valley International School" payable at Patna.

Scholarships & Concessions

TGVIS believes talent should not be limited by financial constraints. We offer merit-based fee concessions for academically outstanding students, sibling discounts for families with more than one child enrolled, and special support for students from economically weaker sections. Contact the admissions office to learn more about eligibility and the application process.
