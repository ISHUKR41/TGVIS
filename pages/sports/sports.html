Our Approach

Sports Are Not Optional

At TGVIS, physical education is a core part of every student's day — not an afterthought. We believe sports teach lessons that classrooms cannot: resilience, teamwork, grace in defeat, and the courage to compete.

Every student from Class 1 to Class 10 participates in structured sports sessions with qualified coaches. Our facilities include a full cricket ground, football field, basketball court, athletics track, and a dedicated indoor games area.

What We Play

Our Sports Programs

Cricket

Full-pitch cricket on our dedicated ground. Students learn batting, bowling, and fielding from trained coaches. Inter-house and inter-school matches are held regularly.

Class 4–10  |  Mon, Wed, Fri

Football

Dedicated football field with coaching focused on passing, teamwork, and tactical awareness. Students participate in district-level tournaments.

Class 3–10  |  Tue, Thu

Basketball

Regulation basketball court with coaching in dribbling, shooting, and game strategy. Inter-house tournaments build competitive spirit.

Class 5–10  |  Tue, Thu

Athletics & Track

100m sprint, 400m race, relay, long jump, high jump, and shot put. Annual Sports Day showcases our best athletes with district-level selection opportunities.

All Classes  |  Daily PE Period

Yoga & Wellness

Morning yoga sessions for strength, flexibility, and mental focus. International Yoga Day celebration with all students. Mindfulness and breathing techniques taught from early classes.

All Classes  |  Every Morning

Indoor Games

Table tennis, chess, carrom, and badminton. These games develop strategic thinking, patience, and concentration. Chess tournaments are held termly for all age groups.

All Classes  |  Friday Activity
