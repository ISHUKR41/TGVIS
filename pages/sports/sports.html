Physical Excellence

Our Sports Programs

At TGVIS, sports are not optional — they are essential. Every student participates in structured physical education with professional coaching.

Cricket

Full-size cricket ground with professional coaching. Regular practice sessions, inter-house matches, and district-level competitions.

Football

Football training on our dedicated field. Building teamwork, strategy, and fitness through the beautiful game.

Basketball

Basketball court with regular training sessions. Inter-house tournaments and participation in district competitions.

Athletics

Track and field events including sprints, relay races, long jump, high jump, and shot put. Annual Sports Day showcase.

Yoga & Meditation

Regular yoga sessions for mental and physical wellness. International Yoga Day celebrations and mindfulness practices.

Indoor Games

Table tennis, chess, carrom, and badminton facilities for developing strategic thinking and hand-eye coordination.
