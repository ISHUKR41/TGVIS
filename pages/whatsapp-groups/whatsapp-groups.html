Important Guidelines

  • Groups are managed by class teachers and school administration
  • Only parents/guardians of enrolled students may join
  • Share only school-related content — no spam, ads, or forwarded messages
  • For private concerns, contact the class teacher directly via school office
  • Group links are updated at the start of each academic session
  • Contact TGVIS Office if your group link is expired or not working
