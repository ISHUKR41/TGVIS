Award Categories

At TGVIS, we believe every achievement deserves recognition. Our comprehensive awards system honours academic, sports, cultural, and character excellence.

Academic Excellence Award

Recognizing class toppers, subject toppers, and students with outstanding academic performance throughout the year.

Sports Achievement Award

Honoring athletes who excel in inter-house, inter-school, and district-level sports competitions.

Cultural Star Award

Celebrating talent in music, dance, drama, art, and other performing and visual arts.

Most Improved Student

Recognizing students who show remarkable improvement in academics, behaviour, or overall personality development.

Character & Values Award

Honouring students who exemplify school values — respect, integrity, compassion, and community service.

Attendance Recognition

Students with 95% or higher attendance throughout the session receive a special Attendance Star certificate and recognition during the Annual Day ceremony.

Inter-House Competition

House Points System

Students are divided into four houses. Every academic, sports, and cultural achievement earns points for their house. The winning house receives the prestigious House Trophy at Annual Day.

Annual Day & Prize Distribution

Every year, our Annual Day celebration is the grand stage where we honour our achievers. Students receive trophies, medals, and certificates from our chief guest in front of the entire school community — parents, teachers, and fellow students. It is a day of pride, joy, and motivation for the entire TGVIS family.
