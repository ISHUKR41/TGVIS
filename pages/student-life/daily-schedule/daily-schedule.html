A Day at TGVIS

Structured Daily Routine

Every day at TGVIS is carefully designed to balance academics, physical activity, creative expression, and personal growth — ensuring our students develop into well-rounded individuals.

7:30 AM

Arrival & Welcome

Students arrive by school bus or personal transport. Campus gates open at 7:30 AM. Duty teachers greet every child with a warm welcome, check uniform compliance, and guide them to their classrooms.

7:45 AM

Morning Assembly

A disciplined assembly featuring prayer, the national anthem, thought of the day, general knowledge quiz, and important announcements. Head Boy/Girl and house captains lead the assembly on rotation.

8:00 – 10:00 AM

Academic Block I (3 Periods)

Three focused academic periods covering core subjects — Mathematics, Science, English, Hindi, or Social Studies. Smart classroom tools including digital boards, multimedia presentations, and interactive quizzes enhance learning.

10:00 AM

Short Break (15 min)

A refreshing 15-minute break for snacks and water. Students can visit the canteen, stretch, and socialize before the next academic block begins.

10:15 – 11:35 AM

Academic Block II (2 Periods)

Two additional periods including lab work (Science/Computer), elective subjects, or language classes. Hands-on experiments and project-based learning keep students engaged and curious.

11:35 AM

Lunch Break (40 min)

A wholesome 40-minute lunch break in the supervised dining area. Students enjoy nutritious home-packed meals, socialize with classmates, and recharge for the afternoon. Hygiene and cleanliness are strictly maintained.

12:15 – 1:35 PM

Afternoon Session (2 Periods)

Dedicated to physical education, sports training, art, music, or dance. Students participate in cricket, football, athletics, yoga, and creative workshops. Active learning keeps energy levels high throughout the day.

1:35 – 2:15 PM

Tuition / Activity Period

Integrated tuition sessions for doubt clearing, extra practice, and revision. On designated days, this period is used for club activities, debating, elocution, quiz preparation, or library time.

2:15 PM

Dispersal

Orderly dispersal with bus students escorted by duty teachers to their designated vehicles. Day scholars are picked up by parents/guardians from the main gate. A safe and supervised journey home for every child!

Important Notes for Parents & Students

  • Students must arrive before 7:45 AM. Late arrivals require a written explanation from the parent.
  • Proper school uniform (including ID card) is mandatory every day. Non-compliance may result in a note to parents.
  • Junk food and outside beverages are not permitted on campus. Students should carry a healthy, home-prepared lunch.
  • Mobile phones and electronic gadgets are strictly prohibited on campus.
  • Saturday schedule (if applicable) may differ — check the weekly timetable for details.
  • Parents are requested to pick up day scholars within 15 minutes of dispersal time.
  • For special events, sports days, or exam schedules, a revised timetable will be shared via WhatsApp group.
