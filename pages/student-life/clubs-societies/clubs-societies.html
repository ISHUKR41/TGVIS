Why Clubs Matter

More Than Just Hobbies

Clubs at TGVIS are where students discover talents they never knew they had. Whether it is winning a debate, coding a game, or leading a plantation drive — these experiences build confidence, friendships, and a genuine love for learning.

Discover New Interests

Try things you have never done before, from robotics to creative writing.

Build Friendships

Work with classmates from different sections and age groups who share your passion.

Earn Recognition

Participate in inter-school competitions, Olympiads, and showcase events on stage.

Getting Started

How to Join

1

Talk to Your Class Teacher

Ask your class teacher about the clubs available for your class. They will explain each club and help you choose.

2

Fill Out the Club Registration Form

Collect a registration form from the student affairs office or download it from the school notice board. Fill it out and submit.

3

Attend Your First Session

Show up every Friday afternoon in your assigned club room. Bring your enthusiasm and an open mind!

Ready to Explore?

Clubs are open to all students. Speak to your class teacher or contact the school office to get started.
