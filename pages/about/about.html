Our Story

A Legacy of Excellence

The Green Valley International School was started with a simple idea — every child in Bihta deserves access to the kind of education that builds real confidence, strong character, and a genuine love for learning.

From our early days as a small primary school, we have grown into a full Nursery to Class 10 institution with dedicated classrooms, science and computer labs, a sports ground, and a library. We also run an integrated tuition programme so students get extra academic support right here on campus — no separate coaching needed.

Today, over 500 students walk through our gates every morning. They come from families across Bihta, Danapur, and greater Patna. What brings them here is straightforward: caring teachers, a clean campus, and results that families can see in their children's growth — not just in marks, but in how they speak, think, and treat others.

Our Foundation

Vision, Mission & Values

Our Vision

To be a beacon of educational excellence that inspires lifelong learning, cultivates global citizens, and empowers every student to reach their highest potential while staying rooted in Indian values and traditions.

Our Mission

To provide a safe, nurturing, and stimulating environment where students receive holistic education — excelling in academics, sports, arts, and character building through innovative teaching methods and dedicated mentorship.

Our Values

Integrity, Respect, Excellence, Compassion, and Innovation form the pillars of our school culture. We believe in nurturing not just intelligent students, but kind, responsible, and courageous human beings.

Milestones & Achievements

Foundation

School Established

The Green Valley International School was founded in Bihta with the vision of bringing quality education to the region.

Growth Phase

Expanded to Class 10th

Successfully expanded from primary education to include middle and secondary school, offering complete Nursery to Class 10th education.

Strong Academic Results

Students began scoring well in board exams, bringing recognition to the school across Bihta and neighbouring areas. Parents started trusting TGVIS for senior classes too.

Introduced smart classrooms, computer labs, science labs, and integrated tuition programs.

TGVIS is now home to over 500 students, 30+ experienced teachers, and a vibrant community of parents who actively participate in school life through PTMs, events, and class WhatsApp groups.
