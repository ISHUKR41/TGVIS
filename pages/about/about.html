Our Story

A Legacy of Excellence

The Green Valley International School was founded with a singular vision — to create a world-class educational institution in Bihta that could nurture young minds and shape responsible, confident, and capable future leaders.

What began as a humble initiative has grown into one of the most trusted schools in the region, serving students from Nursery to Class 10th. Our commitment to academic rigour, holistic development, and moral values has earned us the trust of hundreds of families.

With state-of-the-art facilities, a team of dedicated educators, and an integrated tuition program that sets us apart, TGVIS continues to raise the bar for quality education in Bihar.

Our Foundation

Vision, Mission & Values

Our Vision

To be a beacon of educational excellence that inspires lifelong learning, cultivates global citizens, and empowers every student to reach their highest potential while staying rooted in Indian values and traditions.

Our Mission

To provide a safe, nurturing, and stimulating environment where students receive holistic education — excelling in academics, sports, arts, and character building through innovative teaching methods and dedicated mentorship.

Our Values

Integrity, Respect, Excellence, Compassion, and Innovation form the pillars of our school culture. We believe in nurturing not just intelligent students, but kind, responsible, and courageous human beings.
