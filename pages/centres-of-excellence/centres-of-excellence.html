Our Six Excellence Centres

Centre for Science & Innovation

Three separate laboratories — Physics, Chemistry, and Biology — fully equipped with modern instruments for every CBSE experiment from Class 6 to Class 10. Students do real experiments, not just read about them. The Science Centre also hosts our annual Science Exhibition, where students present working models and original projects.

  • Physics Lab with electrical and optics equipment
  • Chemistry Lab with fume hood and reagent cabinets
  • Biology Lab with microscopes and preserved specimens
  • Olympiad preparation coaching every Friday
  • Annual Science Exhibition open to parents

Centre for Digital Learning

A 30-computer lab with high-speed internet connectivity and licensed software. All classrooms are connected to digital boards that allow teachers to use multimedia presentations, educational videos, and interactive quizzes. The Coding Club meets here every Friday, introducing students to programming basics from Class 5 upward.

  • 30 computers with internet access
  • Smart boards in all classrooms (Class 1–10)
  • Coding Club: Scratch, HTML/CSS, Python basics
  • Digital literacy as a compulsory subject
  • MS Office and typing practice for all students

Centre for Arts & Culture

A dedicated activity hall for visual arts, music, dance, and drama. Students have access to art materials, musical instruments, and a stage for performances. The Arts Centre is home to our Annual Day rehearsals, Independence Day programs, cultural competitions, and the school magazine editorial team.

  • Visual arts: drawing, painting, clay modelling, craft
  • Music: vocals, harmonium, tabla, and guitar
  • Dance: classical, folk, and contemporary
  • Drama and stage performance training
  • School magazine published annually by students

Centre for Sports Excellence

A full-size sports ground with marked cricket pitch, football field, basketball court, and athletics track. Qualified coaches (not just PE teachers) run structured training sessions for each sport. The Sports Centre trains students for inter-school competitions, district championships, and State-level selections.

  • Cricket coaching with professional coaches
  • Football and basketball on marked fields/courts
  • Athletics: sprints, long jump, high jump, shot put
  • Yoga and mindfulness for all classes daily
  • Indoor games: chess, carrom, table tennis

Centre for Language & Communication

A language-focused programme that helps every student — regardless of background — communicate clearly and confidently in English and Hindi. Regular debate sessions, elocution competitions, public speaking workshops, and the Spelling Bee challenge are all run through this centre.

  • Spoken English sessions from Class 3 onwards
  • Inter-house Debate Competition each term
  • Annual Elocution and Spelling Bee contest
  • Hindi creative writing and poetry workshops
  • School library with 5000+ books in English and Hindi

Integrated Tuition Centre (Exclusive to TGVIS)

Unlike most schools, TGVIS runs an in-school tuition programme after regular hours. Students who need extra academic support — or who want to work harder for board exams — can attend supervised sessions without leaving campus. This eliminates the cost and safety concerns of external tuition centres.

  • Available for Class 1 to Class 10 students
  • Subject-wise doubt-clearing by the same teachers
  • Mock tests and board exam preparation
  • Supervised on-campus sessions — no travel needed
  • Significantly more affordable than external coaching
