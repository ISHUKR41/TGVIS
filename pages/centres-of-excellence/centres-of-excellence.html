Our Excellence Centres

Each Centre of Excellence at TGVIS is a dedicated hub where students receive focused training, expert mentorship, and advanced resources.

Centre for Science & Innovation

Fully equipped Physics, Chemistry, and Biology labs. Science exhibitions, Olympiad coaching, and hands-on experiments that make science exciting and tangible for every student.

Centre for Digital Learning

Modern computer lab with coding workshops, digital literacy programs, and smart classroom technology integration across all classes.

Centre for Arts & Culture

Dedicated space for visual arts, performing arts, music, dance, and cultural heritage programs. Annual exhibitions and inter-school competitions.

Centre for Sports Excellence

Professional coaching in cricket, football, basketball, athletics, and yoga. Training for district and state-level competitions.

Centre for Language & Communication

English language lab, Hindi creative writing, debate and public speaking training. Building confident communicators who can express ideas effectively.

Integrated Tuition Centre

Our unique in-school tuition program offering extra academic support, doubt-clearing sessions, mock tests, and personalised coaching for Board exam preparation.
