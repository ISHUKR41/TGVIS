The TGVIS Family

A school is only as strong as the community behind it. At TGVIS, we have built a community where parents are partners, teachers are mentors, alumni are role models, and neighbours are friends.

Every event, every PTM, every WhatsApp update — it all exists so that parents and the school stay in sync, and so that children grow up feeling truly supported.

Parent Partnership

Parent-Teacher Association

The TGVIS Parent-Teacher Association (PTA) is an active body that ensures the school and parents always work in the same direction — towards better outcomes for every child.

The PTA meets quarterly to discuss academic progress, school facilities, safety, events, and any concerns parents may have. All parents are welcome to attend.

What the PTA Does

  • Quarterly meetings with the principal and class teachers to review student progress
  • Parent representation in school decisions about curriculum and events
  • Organising Annual Day, Sports Day, and cultural events with parent volunteers
  • Fundraising for scholarships, library books, and playground equipment
  • Feedback channel for parents to raise concerns directly with management
