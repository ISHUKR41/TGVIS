Our Expectations

What We Look For

  • Genuine Passion for Teaching We want teachers who love their subject and love seeing students understand it. Enthusiasm is non-negotiable.
  • Patience and Empathy Every child learns differently. We need teachers who can adapt and give every student the time they need.
  • Strong Subject Knowledge You should know your subject deeply enough to answer the awkward questions that curious students ask.
  • Punctuality and Professionalism Reliable, punctual, and respectful of the school's values, rules, and the trust parents place in us.
  • Team Player Willing to collaborate with colleagues, participate in school events, and contribute to the overall school community.
