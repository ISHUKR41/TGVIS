Upcoming Events

Here is our event calendar for the current session. Specific dates and times may shift slightly — check with your class teacher or the school WhatsApp group for the latest updates.

15 DEC
Cultural

Annual Day Celebration

Our biggest celebration of the year! Students perform dances, plays, and musical acts on stage. Prizes are given for academics, sports, and co-curricular achievements. Parents and families are invited to enjoy the evening.

10:00 AM - 4:00 PM School Auditorium
20 JAN
Sports

Annual Sports Day

A full day of athletics, races, and team sports! Students compete in 100m dash, relay races, long jump, cricket, football, and tug-of-war. Medals and trophies awarded to winners. Families are welcome to cheer from the stands.

8:00 AM - 2:00 PM Sports Ground
10 FEB
Academic

Science Exhibition

Students from Class 3 to 10 showcase working science models and experiments. Topics range from solar energy to water purification. Judges include local scientists and teachers. Best projects win prizes and certificates.

9:00 AM - 1:00 PM Science Lab & Hall
25 MAR
Meeting

Parent-Teacher Meeting

Meet your child's class teacher one-on-one to discuss academic progress, attendance, and behavior. Report cards are shared during the meeting. Parents can also speak with the Principal for any concerns.

10:00 AM - 1:00 PM Respective Classrooms
26 JAN
National

Republic Day Celebration

Flag hoisting ceremony by the Director, followed by patriotic songs, speeches, and a parade by students in uniform. NCC cadets and Scout Guides lead the march. Sweets are distributed to all students afterward.

8:00 AM - 11:00 AM School Ground
18 NOV
Creative

Inter-House Art Competition

Students compete in painting, sketching, clay modelling, and poster making. Each house — Green, Blue, Yellow, and Red — sends its best artists. Winning artwork is displayed in the school gallery for the rest of the year.

9:00 AM - 12:00 PM Activity Hall
