Our Purpose

Education With Responsibility

At TGVIS, we believe that the true measure of a good education is not just academic performance — it is how a student treats the world around them. Our outreach programs teach children empathy, environmental awareness, and the habit of service.

From Class 4 onwards, every student participates in at least one outreach activity per term. These are not optional add-ons. They are part of what it means to be a TGVIS student.

What We Do

Our Outreach Programs

Plantation Drives

Every year on World Environment Day (June 5th) and on teacher-organised occasions, TGVIS students plant trees in and around the campus and in nearby villages. Students care for their planted trees and track growth. It teaches environmental responsibility in the most direct way possible.

June & October

Village Visits

Senior students (Class 8–10) visit nearby villages under teacher supervision to run awareness programs on hygiene, girl child education, and voter registration. Students prepare and deliver short talks, distribute informational material, and interact with community members. A powerful empathy-building exercise.

Once per semester

Swachh Bharat Campaign

Each year, TGVIS participates in Swachh Bharat Abhiyan. Students clean the school campus, the road outside, and a nearby public area. Waste segregation is taught practically — plastic goes here, organic goes there. Students often bring these habits home to their families.

October 2nd (Gandhi Jayanti)

Charity Collection Drive

Every year before Diwali, TGVIS runs a charity drive where students and families donate used clothes, books, school stationery, and toys. Collected items are donated to underprivileged children in the Bihta area. Students pack, organise, and deliver the donations themselves.

Before Diwali

Water Conservation Awareness

Students in Classes 6–8 run a water-saving awareness campaign every March (World Water Day). They create posters, make short speeches in the assembly, and bring pledges home for parents to sign. A small school action, but it ripples into hundreds of households.

March 22nd (World Water Day)

Health & Hygiene Camps

Partnering with local health workers, TGVIS runs annual health check-up camps for students and — on select days — for parents and community members too. Topics include handwashing, nutrition, dental hygiene, and eye care. Students learn health basics they take home.

Quarterly
