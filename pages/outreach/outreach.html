Our Outreach Programs

At TGVIS, we teach our students that education carries a responsibility to serve others and make the world a better place.

Plantation Drives

Regular tree planting campaigns within and around the campus. Students learn about environmental conservation and their role in protecting nature.

Village Visits

Students visit nearby villages to spread awareness about education, hygiene, and health. Building empathy and social consciousness.

Clean India Campaign

Swachh Bharat initiatives including campus cleaning, waste segregation awareness, and community cleanliness drives.

Charity Drives

Annual charity collections of clothes, books, and school supplies donated to underprivileged children in the community.

Water Conservation

Awareness campaigns and practical initiatives for water conservation, including rainwater harvesting education.

Health Awareness

Health camps, hygiene awareness drives, and nutrition education programs for students and the local community.
