The Director

The Green Valley International School, Bihta

Dear Parents, Students, and Well-Wishers,

It gives me immense pleasure to welcome you to The Green Valley International School — a place where education meets inspiration, and where every child is nurtured to discover their true potential.

When we established TGVIS in Bihta, our vision was clear: to create an educational institution that combines academic excellence with strong moral values, where students are not just prepared for examinations but for life itself. We wanted to build a school where every child, regardless of their background, could receive a world-class education right here in our community.

Over the years, we have witnessed our students grow into confident, responsible, and compassionate individuals. Our dedicated team of 50+ teachers, our modern classrooms with smart boards and digital learning tools, and our unique integrated tuition program have all contributed to creating an environment where learning is a joy.

We believe that every child is unique and deserves individual attention. That is why we maintain optimum class sizes, provide personalised mentoring, and offer a diverse range of academic, sports, and extracurricular activities. From cricket and football on our playground to art and music in our activity rooms — we ensure holistic development.

Our consistent 100% board results are a testament to the hard work of our students and the dedication of our faculty. But for us, success is not just about marks — it is about building character, developing critical thinking skills, and fostering a love for lifelong learning. We want our students to become good human beings first, and successful professionals second.

The integrated tuition program is something we are particularly proud of. Students can avail extra coaching and doubt-clearing sessions right within the school environment, saving parents the hassle and expense of separate tuition centres. This ensures consistent academic support in a familiar and supervised setting.

I invite you to visit our campus, meet our teachers, and experience the TGVIS difference firsthand. Together, let us nurture your child's dreams and shape a brighter future.

With warm regards,

The Director

The Green Valley International School, Bihta
