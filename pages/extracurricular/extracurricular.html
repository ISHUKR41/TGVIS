Our Philosophy

Learning That Goes Beyond the Classroom

Academics build knowledge. Extracurricular activities build the person. At TGVIS, we believe that a student who dances, debates, codes, and volunteers is a stronger, more confident human being than one who only studies.

Every Friday afternoon is dedicated to activity time. Every student from Class 1 to Class 10 participates in at least one extracurricular activity. We believe every child has a talent — our job is to help them find it.

What We Offer

Our Activities

Something for every interest, every personality, every child.

Visual Arts

Drawing, painting, watercolour, charcoal, clay modelling, and mixed media. Students exhibit their work at our Annual Art Exhibition and compete in inter-school art contests.

All Classes Fri Activity Period

Music

Vocal training, harmonium, tabla, and guitar. Students perform at national festivals, Annual Day, and cultural competitions. Choir rehearsals run weekly.

All Classes Tue + Fri

Dance

Classical (Bharatanatyam, Kathak), folk (Bhojpuri, Chhau), and contemporary dance styles. Students perform at Annual Day and cultural competitions across the district.

All Classes Fri Activity Period

Drama & Theatre

Acting, scriptwriting, voice projection, and stage management. TGVIS produces an annual drama and skits for every school celebration. Drama builds confidence like nothing else.

Class 4–10 Fri Activity Period

Debate & Public Speaking

Structured debates, extempore, elocution, and group discussions every term. Inter-house Debate Competition held twice a year. Builds critical thinking and the courage to speak up.

Class 5–10 Alternating Fridays

Quiz & GK

Inter-class and inter-house quiz competitions covering science, history, geography, current affairs, and Indian culture. Prepares students for competitive exams and broadens their world view.

Class 3–10 Monthly Competitions

Coding Club

Scratch visual programming for beginners, then HTML/CSS, and basic Python. Students build simple websites and apps. The next generation of tech leaders starts here, in Class 5.

Class 5–10 Fri Activity Period

Eco Club

Plantation drives, waste segregation, energy conservation campaigns, and environment awareness posters. The Eco Club leads our annual Clean India initiative and gets students thinking about sustainability.

Class 3–10 Monthly Activities

Creative Writing

Essay writing, short stories, poetry, and the school magazine. Students learn to express ideas clearly, think creatively, and develop a writing habit that serves them for life.

Class 4–10 Fri Activity Period
The Big Events

Annual Showcases

These are the events where extracurricular talent takes centre stage in front of parents and the whole school.

Annual Day

School celebrations may include dance, drama, music, and recognition. Dates, participation, and visitor access are confirmed for each session.

December every year

Art Exhibition

Student artwork displayed in the school hall. Parents can view and purchase select pieces, with proceeds going to the scholarship fund.

November every year

Science Exhibition

Students present working models, experiments, and research projects. Open to parents and visitors. District-level winners get to represent TGVIS.

January every year

Inter-House Debate Final

The grand debate final between all four houses. Topics cover current affairs, social issues, and science. Parents are invited to watch and vote.

Get Started

How to Join an Activity

  1. 1
    Choose your activity
    Browse the activities above and decide which one excites you most. You can join up to two activities.
  2. 2
    Tell your class teacher
    Your class teacher will add your name to the activity register. No formal application needed.
  3. 3
    Show up every Friday
    Friday afternoon is your dedicated activity time. Come prepared, participate fully, and most importantly — have fun.
