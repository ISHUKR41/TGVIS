Creative Pursuits

We believe every child has a unique talent. Our extracurricular programs help students discover and nurture their creative passions.

Visual Arts

Drawing, painting, sketching, clay modelling, and craft. Regular art exhibitions and inter-school competitions.

Music

Vocal and instrumental music training. Performances at school events, national festivals, and cultural programmes.

Dance

Classical, folk, and contemporary dance training. Students perform at Annual Day, cultural fests, and community events.

Drama & Theatre

Acting, scriptwriting, and stagecraft. Annual drama productions and skits that build confidence and communication skills.

Debate & Public Speaking

Regular debate sessions, extempore, elocution, and Model United Nations to develop critical thinking and oratory skills.

Quiz & Knowledge

Inter-class and inter-house quiz competitions on general knowledge, science, current affairs, and Indian heritage.

Creative Writing

Essay writing, poetry, storytelling, and school magazine contributions. Nurturing the next generation of writers and thinkers.

Coding Club

Introduction to programming, Scratch, and basic web development for interested students. Building digital literacy from an early age.

Eco Club

Environmental awareness, plantation drives, waste management, and sustainability projects led by passionate student eco-warriors.
