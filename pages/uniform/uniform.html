Uniform Guidelines

Summer Uniform

Boys

  • White half-sleeve shirt with school logo
  • Green trousers, black shoes, and white socks
  • School belt and ID card

Girls

  • White half-sleeve shirt with school logo
  • Green skirt or salwar, black shoes, and white socks
  • School belt, ID card, and green ribbon

Winter Uniform

Boys:
• White full-sleeve shirt
• Green trousers
• Green school sweater/blazer with logo
• Black shoes with green socks

Girls:
• White full-sleeve shirt
• Green skirt/salwar
• Green school sweater/blazer with logo
• Black shoes with green socks

Sports Uniform

All Students:
• White sports T-shirt with school logo
• Green track pants
• White sports shoes with white socks
• House colour T-shirt for Sports Day

Note:
Sports uniform to be worn on sports days and physical education periods only.

Guidelines

Uniform Rules

  • All students must wear the complete school uniform with ID card every day.
  • Uniforms should be clean, ironed, and well-maintained at all times.
  • No fancy accessories, jewellery, or coloured nail polish allowed.
  • Boys must have neat, short hair. Girls must tie hair with green ribbons.
  • Uniforms are available at the school uniform counter. Contact the office for details.

Where to Buy Uniforms

School uniforms are available at the TGVIS uniform counter on campus. Contact the office for pricing, sizes, and availability.
